## Why is Accessibility Important, a Developer's Opinion

> Accessibility is everyones responsibility
> -- Somebody said that

This means everyone, from project requests to the outline of the wireframes to the Figma files to the code, everyone needs to consider the usability of the website. This sounds obvious but it happens.

The designer gives you a beautiful component that will not transfer well from desktop to mobile, there is no way the developer can do their job of making an accessible website for everyone.

## Tips

Writing good HTML is step 1. If you can't write clean HTML then you have a problem. Plain HTML (no CSS) is the most accessible way to make a website.
