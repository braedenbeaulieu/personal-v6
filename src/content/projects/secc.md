South Essex Community Council (SECC) has delivered community programs and social services in Windsor-Essex since 1973, with a focus on newcomer support, youth programs, and senior services. The WordPress site acts as a community hub — connecting residents to available programs, room rentals, and resources aligned with the organization's "Helping People. Improving Lives." mandate.
