EVcareers.ca is a job-matching platform dedicated to the electric vehicle industry in Windsor-Essex. The WordPress site features job listings, a skills library with over 29,000 categorized terms, and job seeker profiles. A partnership among Invest WindsorEssex, Workforce WindsorEssex, WEtech Alliance, the University of Windsor, and St. Clair College.
