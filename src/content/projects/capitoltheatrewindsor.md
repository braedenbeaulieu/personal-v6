Capitol Theatre Windsor is a multi-space performing arts venue in downtown Windsor, Ontario. The WordPress site handles event listings, venue rental inquiries, and ticketing integrations for a facility that operates as a cultural anchor in the city — hosting everything from Windsor Symphony Orchestra concerts to film screenings and private events.
