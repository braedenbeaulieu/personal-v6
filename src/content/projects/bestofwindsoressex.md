Best of Windsor-Essex is a community-driven awards program that spotlights the region's top businesses and experiences through public voting. The WordPress site handles annual award cycles across categories like Food, Arts & Entertainment, Stay, and Outdoor Adventure — serving as both a regional directory and a recognition platform for local businesses.
