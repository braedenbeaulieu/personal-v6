The Grove is a boutique hospitality brand operating two distinct properties in Essex County: The Grove Hotel in downtown Kingsville and The Grove Motel in Colchester on Lake Erie. The WordPress site showcases their uniquely designed rooms, on-site café, and booking options — representing a brand rated in the top 10% of hotels worldwide on TripAdvisor for seven consecutive years.
