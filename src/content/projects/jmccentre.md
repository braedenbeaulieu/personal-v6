John McGivney Children's Centre (JMCC) has served Windsor-Essex since 1978, providing rehabilitation and disability services for children and youth. The WordPress site supports the centre's mission of family-centred care — surfacing programs, resources, and referral pathways for families navigating disability services in the region.
