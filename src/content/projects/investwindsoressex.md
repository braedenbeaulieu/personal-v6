Invest WindsorEssex is a not-for-profit economic development agency supported by the City of Windsor and County of Essex. The WordPress site serves as the primary touchpoint for companies and entrepreneurs seeking to establish or grow in the Windsor-Essex area, offering sector information, investment resources, and confidential expert assistance.
