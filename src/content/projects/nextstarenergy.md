NextStar Energy is Canada's first large-scale lithium-ion battery manufacturer, engineering advanced battery cells and module systems for electric vehicles and energy storage. The WordPress site serves the company's corporate presence as it scales commercial production in Windsor — a landmark facility in North America's clean energy transition.
