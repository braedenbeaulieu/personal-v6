The Windsor Symphony Orchestra (WSO) is Windsor's professional orchestra, based at Capitol Theatre downtown. The WordPress site supports their full programming — concert ticketing, community education initiatives, Youth Orchestras, a Community Orchestra, and the WSO Chorus — reflecting an organization committed to making live music accessible across Windsor-Essex.
